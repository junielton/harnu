Context and decisions

Problem. The Stewards block sits under the folder groups as a flex-none section with no cap. With 13 stewards it eats the whole column and the worker list scrolls down to two rows.

Proposal.

  • Stewards gets the Up next header: chevron + 10px uppercase title + a one-line summary at the right. Clicking it toggles the block.
  • Collapsed by default, persisted per viewer (om2tab.scheduler.stewardsCollapsed, same as Up next). Collapsed, it costs one 32px line.
  • The summary says what matters without opening: 13 · 1 running · 10 awaiting close.
  • Open, the rows scroll inside a capped height (~6 rows). The worker list keeps the rest of the column.
  • A steward whose mission is delivered and waiting for your close reads Awaiting close at the right, and a hint under the rows says closing the mission retires it. That's why there are 13.

New token. The cap is a new dimension: --sched-stewards-max-h: 232px in themes.css + design.md §6 "Scheduler → Stewards section", in the same change.

Current state: the Stewards block is an uncapped section under the folder groups, so with 13 stewards it fills the whole 340px list column and leaves the worker list two rows.
