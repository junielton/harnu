Context and decisions

The concept in one line: Mission → Milestone → Step, and the Step is the board card itself (one pull request). There is one state flow, and everything that waits on you goes to one list.

Today v4
Campaign → Chapter → Mission → Step → Check (5 levels) Mission → Milestone (optional) → Step (3 levels)
The mission's steps and the board's cards (two trackers) The step is the card; the mission groups the cards
6 state vocabularies To do · Running · Waiting for review · Done, and Blocked is a mark
3 "You" lists plus the Your move queue One list, inside the pane, for this session's mission
Gates + checks + proof + verdict The step's acceptance criteria: the automatic part (pull request, CI, main, a second session) plus your own checks
Pill: "Ch. 3/3 · Step 10/10 · 4 for you" Pill: "4/17 · 3 for you", for this session's mission
A popover over the screen, with everything inside A pane in the helper stack that follows the session and shows only the path: Done → Now → Next

The mission is durable, the session is disposable: the mission lives in the folder with its plan, brief, history and the list of sessions that worked on it. A session only touches the mission it is attached to, with confirmation. Replacing the leading session with a new one is always the operator's decision; Harnu only notices and offers. Acceptance criteria stay on the step.

What stays the same (principles): only the server counts progress; every door is the operator's (approve, tick, close); closing is never blocked; "done" comes from facts (gates), never from the agent's report.

Discarded by the operator: the kanban view of a mission and the cross-mission "My work" queue. The pane is the only screen; what waits on you appears inside the session's own mission.

Gone: merge batch, side quests, radiant, "you are here", the tile map and the fixed step "Delivered and verified". The end criterion is the mission's Done when, and you confirm it in "Close mission…".

Settled in the spec: chapters that are separate missions today are read as milestones of one mission and converted by copy on first write; "To do" covers the board's Backlog and Ready; the steward is retired. Each of these is a call made for the operator, to review, in the spec's Open decisions table.

The only screen. The badge opens and closes the pane, and the pane follows the session (click the sessions in the sidebar). Top to bottom: a progress bar with the count inside it, one next step, and the tree milestone ▸ step ▸ acceptance criteria. waiting the rest of what waits on you, in place · step-open the criteria of a step · step-blocked the blocker and its action · all-steps every step of the milestone · ask · sessions · handoff-suggest / handoff-confirm · simple a small task from the tracker, with no milestones and the criteria pulled from the task · simple-signoff other people's sign-offs, which you mark · big a real 10-milestone mission (neutral data): three built milestones waiting for review at once, the bar counting milestones · big-waiting the 5 things that wait · no-mission · attach-request.
