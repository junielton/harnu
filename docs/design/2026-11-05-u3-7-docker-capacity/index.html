Context and decisions

U3 of the Autonomous Cleanup spec makes Cleanup watch Docker's bridge-network slots, pause idle stacks, and remove garbage on its own. These three screens are the operator-facing surface: what the Docker card shows, the one-time address-pool advice, and the stale-volume warning in Activity. Approve them one by one.

Decisions taken here: the existing card structure and its three blocks stay as they are; two blocks lead the grid and one full-width list closes it; the advice dialog is its own screen (the spec makes it a dedicated guided dialog), reached from a one-time callout on the card; the stale-volume warning reuses the Activity row anatomy (kind bar, optional action pill, grouped entry for several).

Copy (design.md §8: short labels, no trailing period; "Paused" in the UI, never "parked"):
