# T389 plan review

**Date:** 2026-10-02 · **Reviewer:** independent (did not write the plans) · **Scope:** `docs/plans/T389-companion-mod/` (`00-master-plan.md`, `P1-sensor.md` to `P5-retirement.md`) against `docs/specs/T389-companion-mod/` (master `00-master.md` sections 4, 5, 12 and 13, the contract, and the 19 wave specs). Decisions already taken by the orchestrator (Gate K after P1 with P2W1 buildable before it, the WIP ceiling of 4 to 5, join rebases for the two-parent waves with P2W5 core between P2W2 and the P2W3/P2W4 fan-out, evidence and addenda paths) were treated as fixed.

## 1. Method

1. **Structure and DAG.** Read the master plan and the five phase plans in full; compared every wave, slice, base branch, join and batch with master spec section 4 (waves), section 5 (dependency table, parallel sets) and section 12 (interface owners and consumers), and with the "Depends on / Base branch" lines of each wave spec. Checked that every interface a wave consumes is owned by an ancestor of its base, and that the merge order of master plan section 6 never needs a rebase of an already merged branch.
2. **AC coverage.** Extracted every `AC-P<p>W<w>-<n>` definition from the 19 specs with a script (line pattern `AC-… [method]`), extracted every AC id the phase plans cite (expanding "1 to 36", "-11, -12" and the bare `AC-35` form inside a wave section), and diffed both ways. Did the same for `LV-` recipes, and for the AC to LV pairs named in each AC's `Evidence:`. Repeated the diff restricted to step-table rows to find ACs that appear only in a boot packet; the residual hits were parser limits on parenthetical lists and were checked by hand.
3. **Test-first.** For each AC whose method is `unit`, `contract` or `mod-test`, checked that the plan names it in a "Tests first" cell (or a "Tests first:" sentence for slice prose) before the code step.
4. **Files.** Extracted every path-like token from the plans and resolved it against the tracked tree; the tokens not found were separated into "new in this wave" and "cited as existing". Spot-checked the cited symbols (`resolveArmTarget` at `tool-handlers.ts:2208`, `handleBridgeEvent`, `RESPONDER_DEADLINE_MS`, `removeGuardHookRegistration`, the T200 and T215 specs, `local-pipeline.sh` flags).
5. **Boot packets.** Counted words in all 28 packets (largest: 236), checked the nine mandatory fields (objective, setup, read first, may touch, satisfy, skills, out of scope, return, autonomy) and searched for merge, push and flip wording.
6. **Docs contracts.** Compared master spec section 4 flags and each spec's section 12 table with the "Docs" column of master plan section 4 and the steps of the phase plans.
7. **Hygiene.** Ran the two key patterns, the `Workspace/` pattern and a home-path pattern of `tests/no-client-identifiers.test.ts` over every new doc (the test itself only sees tracked files, and these are untracked).
