Context and decisions

A Mission today is one quest: one declared end and a flat list of steps. A real 93-hour delivery needed five missions opened by hand, 26 worktrees, 73 sessions and at least eight unplanned detours that had nowhere to live. The operator ended up asking the orchestrator to hand-draw the campaign on a canvas while the pill read "Step 1 of 4".

Concepts under test: Campaign (chapters = missions, v3 untouched), side quests (required: false, discovered-from), gates from facts (PR · CI · main · verifier), locked-until-ready, radiant steps (48 / 88), decision nodes, an owed-to-you lane aged by risk, a batch merge door, and the Steward (a headless agent bound to the mission).

Four screens, one sample data set (a scrubbed content migration for the fictional client Acme). Shared anatomy and parts come from design.md §6 "Mission progress"; the screens were picked from five reviewed variants (v1 popovers and v4 map) and approved on 2026-10-30. The spec is spec.md in this folder.
